I launched [Lawman](https://github.com/JonPurvis/lawman) earlier this year to make it easy to bring the joy or [PestPHP](https://pestphp.com/) architecture testing, to [SaloonPHP](https://docs.saloon.dev/). I've just seen that it's gotten past it's first 1000 downloads, which I'm really pleased about!

Lawman scratched my own itch as I wanted to ensure my application adhered to architectural rules, no matter who was working on the project. I feel like Lawman solves that problem.

There's a couple of open issues I want to tackle with Lawman, both to do with just adding a couple of extra Expectations. Namely for Plugins and Request Pagination.

Here's to the next 1000! 🍻
