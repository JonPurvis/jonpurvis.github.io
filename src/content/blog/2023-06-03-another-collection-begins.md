I've got to thank [Steve King](https://twitter.com/JustSteveKing) for getting me started on collecting these Elephpants, his collection is so cool! There's around 70 or so to get, and my first one has already arrived.

My very first one is the Sunny Nexmo Elephpant all the way from the USA! I've already got 2 more on the way 🤣 There's some that I'd like more than the others, below are my absolute favourites that I really want to try and get for my collection.

I'll be sure to update my blog whenever I acquire any new ones. They don't release that often so it'll be good to collect these at a much slower pace than some of the previous things I've collected!

Happy hunting!
