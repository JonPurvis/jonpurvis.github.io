I've just added Recommendations to this website, which is a native Ghost feature, allowing you to recommend your favourite websites with your audience.

I read a lot of blogs within the tech space and I wanted a way of highlighting my favourites, so that readers of this blog can also check out other blogs too. I'll try to keep it to around 5 at a time as I don't want the list to be overwhelming, but my current recommendations have a wide range of topics from Laravel, Security, API's and more.

The great thing about Ghost Recommendations is it doesn't have to be only Ghost websites, in fact, at the time of writing this, the only Ghost website I'm recommending is:
