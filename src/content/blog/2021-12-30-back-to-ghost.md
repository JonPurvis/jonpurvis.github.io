It's almost a new year and for 2022 I fancied creating and trying to post regularly on a blog.

The obvious choice of platform was of course, Ghost. I've used Ghost many times before since version 1.0. Ghost was also where I first dabbled in the world of Open Source development and I submitted a [small number of fixes/improvements](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3ACriticalRespawn+is%3Aclosed) to the platform, between versions 1.21.6 and 1.24.6.

Lots of changes since then though! I did briefly have a Ghost 3.0 site in another attempt to blog, but I eventually stopped that and closed the droplet down. This time though, I'm determined to last the entirety of 2022.

Ghost has a bunch of features I'll never really use such as Paid Memberships, thankfully though you can run a blog without using that stuff. You can subscribe though to be notified of any new posts that are created!

I'm not going to set myself any rules with this, I'll just post whenever the need arises but the general theme will most likely be; gaming, web development and food (because who doesn't love food 🍔).
