I got back from Laravel Live 2026 yesterday morning and was able to make a couple of additions to my Herd from the conference:

*   Eric the Red: This is the ElePHPant that is for Laravel Live Denmark this year. I was able to reach out to [Kasper Hartwich](https://x.com/kasperh) who is the main organiser of that conference and arrange a trade for a Sylius (Green) as I had 2 duplicates, so it was the perfect opportunity to get rid of one!
*   Sentaur: I realised ahead of time that Sentry were a sponsor, and I thought they may have a booth, so I packed a spare Enfys (Rainbow) in hopes of sparking up a trade. I was able to trade one for a Sentaur as they had a booth and has ElePHPants! Towards the end of the conference, I was able to snag a 2nd Sentaur to take back for my team! Very cool of the Sentry team for allowing this!

<aside class="callout callout-info">
<strong>Note</strong>
<p>Turns out, the Sentaur offered at this conference is a different generation to the one from Laracon US. This one is a darker purple and has different coloured headphones!</p>
</aside>
