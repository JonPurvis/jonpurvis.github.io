I had a fantastic day at Elvington Airfield Track Day today! This is the 2nd track day I've done, with the first one being Tockwith, which is basically on the other side of York.

I went down with 2 friends and we spend several hours at the track, and were able to drive some fantastic cars, and watch even more!

I got the chance to drive 2 fantastic cars, one of which is my favourite car of all time; the Porsche 911 GT3 RS and I also drove the Aston Martin DB9.

Unfortunately I didn't actually get many photos or videos this time round, I think I was too focused on enjoying the moment. That said, the cover photo of this post is the Porsche I was able to drive, just look at the green! 😍

We're going to try and do this somewhat regularly at different tracks around the UK, so I'm looking forward to it becoming a regular thing!
