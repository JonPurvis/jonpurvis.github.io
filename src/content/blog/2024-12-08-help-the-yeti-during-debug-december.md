[Sentry](https://sentry.io/welcome/) have a pretty cool challenge running for December called [Debug December](https://www.debugdecember.com/intro), where every day, you can complete a code challenge for a chance to win prizes along the way.

Your task is to help the Yeti climb the mountain by lighting up the path by completing a challenge each day. Upon completion, a light is lit and the path opens up a little bit more for Yeti.

So far, it hasn't been too bad and I'm only a day or two behind, but I'll catch up before the month is over! To give you a bit of a taster, below is a screenshot from Day #7. You can see the code is on the left hand side and the error is on the right hand side, your job is to fix the code on the left, based on the error on the right and then click "Run" in the top right. Providing you've fixed the code properly, then the light will be lit for Yeti!

The prizes are decent, but it's also a fun challenge to do even if you don't win. The prizes are:

*   Day 6, 24 T-Shirts up for grabs
*   Day 12, 10 Hoodies up for grabs
*   Day 18, 5 Airpods up for grabs
*   Day 24, 1 PS5 up for grabs

Good luck with the challenge! You can find out more information about Debug December by [clicking here](https://www.debugdecember.com/challenges/2024).
