For me, the talks were only half of the conference. During each break, providing you put yourself out there to network, you can enjoy some really engaging and thought provoking conversations. I still remember how much my mind was racing with ideas on the train back home after the conference!

This has got me thinking, should I attend Laravel Live UK? I've still got time to decide and I loved the atmosphere of PHPUK so why wouldn't I go?e Based on the speaker line up so far on the [website](https://laravellive.uk/), it's set to be a fantastic conference and I already know a bunch of people that are heading there.
