Thanks to my Open Source contributions on [Elephant.me](https://elephpant.me/), I knew that [Thomas Eiling](https://x.com/TEiling88) had an Elephpant that I was after for my own herd, PHPartner. This Elephpant is a somewhat recent one, having released in 2022.

I sent Thomas a Tweet asking how I was best going around acquiring one and he was kind enough to send me a DM asking for my address so he could sent one over 😍

The kindness of the Elephpant community is amazing! A couple weeks later, it arrived in the post and I was able to cross another Elephpant off my list!
