I'm changing it up this year as the biggest mistake I made last year is doing the whole trip in one day, that meant waking up to catch a 5:30am train and then not getting back home until after midnight! It's safe to say, I was exhausted!

This year I'm going to travel down the night before and do a one night stay over in a hotel. I'll most likely get the train down after work and get to London for 9-10pm. This means I can wake up, have breakfast, get a shower and be fresh and wide awake for the day!

I'll be sure to post my thoughts about the conference afterwards!
