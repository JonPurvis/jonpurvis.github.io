[Jetbrains](https://www.jetbrains.com/) has recently announced that it's hosting an event called PHPVerse on June 17th, and it's a free virtual event to celebrate 30 years of PHP!

It falls on a Tuesday, so hopefully I can watch the stream during work. If not, I'm sure recordings of it will end up on Youtube. It will feature talks from some big names in the language, such:

*   Taylor Otwell
*   Roman Pronskiy
*   Sara Golemon
*   Derick Rethans

It will feature talks about FrankenPHP, Symfony, Laravel, AI, The PHP Foundation and lots more! There's also a chance to win some merch!
