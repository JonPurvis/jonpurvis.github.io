I've been collecting Elephpants for a while now and I've gotten a fair few duplicates in that time, which I don't mind because I put them in my "trade fodder" pile. That pile came into use recently I was able to set up a trade (thanks to [https://elephpant.me/](https://elephpant.me/)) with [Sebastian Feldmann](https://x.com/movetodevnull), creator of [CaptainHookPHP](https://x.com/CaptainHookPHP).

Sebastian was after a PHP Architect "Archie" Elephpant, and I had 3 duplicates sat in my hammock not doing anything. He had a JePHPry Elephpant that I needed for my collection, so we organised a trade. Within a week, we were both able to add an extra Elephpant to our herds!

I'm hoping to organise another trade very soon... 👀
