Earlier this week I tagged v3.15 of Profanify! This release contains support for the Danish language which was added by [Bertel Torp](https://github.com/berteltorp).

If you don't know what Profanify is, it's a Profanity checker plugin for [PestPHP](https://pestphp.com/) and is just a `composer install` away!

It's always fantastic to see extra contributors getting involved with Profanify and it's good to see that even after almost 1 year after the initial release, it's still getting used.
