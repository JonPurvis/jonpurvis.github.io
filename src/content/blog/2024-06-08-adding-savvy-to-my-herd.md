The Herd definitely seems to have grown a lot recently, my Elephpant hammock is pretty much full! That being said, it hasn't stopped me from acquiring another addition to the Herd, this time I'm welcoming Savvy!

Savvy is an Elephpant sponsored by [Safefive](https://safefive.de/en/home/) and I'll be honest, I was totally not expecting to actually get one off the back of my comment on [this PR](https://github.com/jgrossi/elephpant.me/pull/209) when I asked about how I would acquire one.

Fabian was very generous in hooking me up with one and after an email exchange of details and a couple of weeks waiting, I finally had Savvy to hand and was able to add him to the Herd!
