It's very rare that an ElePHPant gets put up for sale on eBay, even rarer when it's one I still need for my collection!

I spotted a seller on eBay selling around 20 ElePHPants as one lot, so I send him a message asking if he'd split the lot, once I'd worked out the only one I needed was Chloe, and offered him what was charging for each one in the lot. He replied and said he'd consider splitting if time goes buy with no sale.

I secretly (selfishly) hoped they wouldn't sell as I really wanted Chloe for my collection! After a month of being live and not selling, I logged onto eBay one day and noticed that the lot had been split into 1 item listings. So I purchased the one I needed as quick as I could and it now sits with the rest of the herd!

There must've been other people doing the same thing I did as when I checked last, there were only 5 listings still active!
