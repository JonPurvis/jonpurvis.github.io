I've always had an interest in cars, so every so often my friends and I attend track days to experience cars that we wouldn't usually get the chance to drive. It's a lot of fun, and honestly a bit addictive once you've had a go.

The first one we did was at Tockwith (I never got round to writing that one up), where I drove a Porsche 911 Spyder, a Subaru Impreza STI, a Nissan GT-R, and even a BMW police interceptor. You might remember I wrote about [Elvington Airfield](/elvington-airfield-track-day/) though, where I got to drive my favourite car of all time, the Porsche 911 GT3 RS, plus an Aston Martin DB9.

Rather than trying to remember them all in my head (which, let's be honest, I was already failing at), I've added a **Cars** page to the site. It's a simple catalog with photos and names, and I'll keep updating it as we get out to more tracks.
