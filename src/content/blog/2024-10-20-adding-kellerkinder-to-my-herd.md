I'm happy to be able to add another Elephpant to my herd! This time I'm adding Kellerkinder, which originally released back in 2021.

This was another trade that I was able to organise via [https://elephpant.me/](https://elephpant.me/) and I traded with [Stephan Hochdoerfer](https://x.com/shochdoerfer) as he was after a Umoja Elephpant which I had a couple of duplicates of!

Not only did Stephan send the Elephpant, he also included a bunch of awesome stickers including [CaptainHookPHP](https://captainhookphp.github.io/captainhook/) and [Longhorn PHP](https://www.longhornphp.com/).

I currently don't have any more ongoing trades but I'll shortly be looking to do some trading, hopefully I can get some new additions!
