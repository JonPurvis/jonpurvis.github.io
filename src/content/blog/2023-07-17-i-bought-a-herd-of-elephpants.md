Some of you may know that this year, I started collecting Elephpants. I just find them so cool! I was planning to get a handful and then grow my herd slowly as there's not like 100's to collect, in fact there's about 70.

I was scrolling through Twitter and noticed that [Vincent Pontier](https://twitter.com/Elroubio), the godfather of Elephpants, who created the official PHP Elephpant mascot back in 1998, had tweeted about someone who was looking to sell their herd.

> (𝟏/𝟑) | It is a historic day! Yes, I said historic. I am addressing all elephpant collectors. The news I am about to announce is not likely to haPHPen again. So, do not miss this unique opportunity and take the time to read the following lines [#php](https://twitter.com/hashtag/php?src=hash&ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw) [@official\_php](https://twitter.com/official_php?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw) [@MarkWalravens](https://twitter.com/MarkWalravens?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw) [pic.twitter.com/UpTrXfKNO3](https://t.co/UpTrXfKNO3)
> 
> — Vincent PONTIER (@Elroubio) [July 17, 2023](https://twitter.com/Elroubio/status/1681036591593078785?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)

I hesitated for about 30 minutes and then send the seller, [Mark Walravens](https://twitter.com/MarkWalravens) a message, expressing my interest. I hesitated some more but then something in my mind came right to the front and I just though "how often does a full herd come up for sale?"

So I bought it...

Yep, I now had 21 more Elephpants to look after 🤣 Now a couple of them, I already had so I can use them for trades, however 19 of them were totally new to my collection. Most of them are *extremely* hard to find.

Do I regret it? Nope! Although they will have to live in a box for the time being as I don't have a proper display set up yet.
