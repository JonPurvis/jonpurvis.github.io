## Hey, I'm Jon! 👋

I’m a Senior Software Engineer at Visualsoft / VS Labs, where we’re on a mission to build the best apps on the marketplace. As a PHP developer, my main framework of choice is Laravel.

Beyond my day job, I’m deeply embedded in the tech community as a developer, open-source maintainer, and conference goer.

### 💻 Open Source & Community

I love giving back to the ecosystem. My open-source packages have been downloaded over **15,000,000 times**, and my work has been featured multiple times on *Laravel News*.

*   **Contributions**: I actively contribute to major projects like PestPHP, SaloonPHP, and ElePHPant.me.
*   **My Packages**: You can check out the tools I build and maintain over on my [Packages](/packages/) page.
*   **Events:** I’m an avid conference goer. You’ll often find me at *PHP UK*, *Laravel Live UK*, and various local meetups. If you see me, come say hey, I'd love to chat!

### 🕹️ Beyond the Screen

When I'm not writing code, I'm usually listening to rock and heavy metal music or diving into a video game.

I'll try almost any gaming genre, except sports games, where I've officially accepted defeat. You’ll usually find me on my Xbox Series X, but I also lose time to *Hearthstone* and *Warcraft Rumble* on my phone, or *World of Tanks* on my MacBook.

### 🌲 Life Outside of Tech

When it's time to completely unplug, I have a few favourite ways to spend my time:

*   **Food:** I love scouting out new local eateries. Indian, Italian, and Chinese are my go-tos (honourable mention to pretty much anything involving chicken).
*   **Geocaching:** I get out at least once a week to hunt for caches, starting close to home and gradually working my way further afield.
*   **Sydney:** Last but definitely not least, I love taking my Dachshund, Sydney, out for walks.
